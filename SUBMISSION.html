BarkPass

What I built

Dog owners notice small changes every day, but those observations usually disappear into photos, scattered notes and memory. BarkPass turns the easiest existing habit, taking a photo, into a repeatable wellness check-in without pretending to diagnose a condition or replace a veterinarian.

An owner creates a profile for any dog and uploads a photo or short video. Gemini returns a strict structured read of visible mood, energy, posture and flags. ElevenLabs turns the result into a short first-person voice note. Snowflake stores dog-scoped check-ins and grounds natural-language trend answers in queried rows. Solana provides a portable devnet passport plus a Phantom-approved shelter-tip rail.

Bruno is the editorial example, not a hardcoded user. Every new BarkPass receives its own dog ID, profile, check-ins, Snowflake query scope and passport metadata.

Fast judge path

  1. Open the one-click judge demo.
  2. Ask the prefilled history question and inspect seven underlying energy values.
  3. Upload a fresh dog photo to run the visual-read flow, then play the voice note.
  4. Open Passport and follow the verified Solana devnet mint link.
  5. Open clean onboarding to create a BarkPass for another dog.

The public deployment is secret-free and labels its local fallbacks. Live sponsor-provider verification is documented in the repository so unrestricted endpoints cannot burn billable credentials.

How the sponsor stack works

Google AI

The browser downsizes a selected photo or extracts one video frame before calling the backend. Gemini receives a non-diagnostic behavior-observer instruction and a strict JSON schema for mood, energy from 1 to 10, posture notes, visible flags and confidence. Five real-photo acceptance tests produced four distinct moods and energy values from 4 to 9.

ElevenLabs

BarkPass converts the structured read into a short line spoken from the dog's perspective. ElevenLabs returns an MP3 that plays inline without a refresh. The measured combined Gemini and ElevenLabs provider path stayed below the 15-second target.

Snowflake

Profiles and check-ins are merged into dog-scoped tables through a least-privilege service user with key-pair authentication. History answers are calculated from rows queried for the requested dog ID, so the interface can show both the summary and the underlying values. A live three-row acceptance set returned an average of 7.0 and a range from 6 to 8, matching the direct worksheet aggregate.

Solana

Metaplex mints dog-specific passport metadata on devnet. The shelter-tip endpoint prepares a fixed 0.01 devnet SOL transaction that the connected Phantom wallet must approve. Luna's verified BarkPass NFT has supply one and a finalized mint transaction.

Known limitations

  • Visual observations depend on photo quality and are not veterinary advice.
  • Profile photos and fallback history stay in the current browser because accounts were out of scope for the weekend.
  • Solana actions use devnet and carry no real monetary value.
